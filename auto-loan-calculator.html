Understanding your car payment

Your auto loan payment depends on the price, sales tax, down payment, trade-in, interest rate and term. Longer terms lower the monthly payment but increase the total interest you pay.

The 20/4/10 rule

A popular guideline suggests putting 20% down, financing for no more than 4 years, and keeping total car costs (payment, insurance, fuel) under 10% of your gross income.

Smart buying tips

Get pre-approved by a bank or credit union before visiting a dealer so you can compare offers. Negotiate the vehicle price separately from financing, and avoid loan terms over 72 months, which can leave you owing more than the car is worth.
