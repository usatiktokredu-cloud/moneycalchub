How loan payments work

Most personal loans are installment loans: you borrow a fixed amount and repay it in equal monthly payments. Early payments are mostly interest, while later payments go mostly toward principal.

Ways to pay less interest

Choose the shortest term you can comfortably afford, compare the APR (not just the rate) across lenders, and check for origination fees. Making even one extra payment per year can shorten your loan and cut interest. Always confirm there is no prepayment penalty first.

Before you borrow

Check your credit score, compare at least three offers, and make sure the payment fits within your monthly budget with room to spare.
