See how inflation reduces the buying power of your money over time.

How inflation affects you

Inflation is the general rise in prices over time. When prices go up, each dollar buys less than before, which is why money sitting in a low-interest account can lose buying power.

Historical context

U.S. inflation has averaged roughly 3% per year over the long term, though individual years vary widely. This calculator lets you test any rate you like.

Protecting your money

Consider savings vehicles whose returns keep pace with inflation, such as high-yield savings accounts, I bonds or diversified investments, depending on your goals and risk tolerance. This is general information, not personal financial advice.
