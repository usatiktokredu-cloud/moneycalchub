What is compound interest?

Compound interest means you earn interest on your original money and on the interest it has already earned. Over long periods this snowball effect can make a big difference.

The power of starting early

Because growth builds on itself, time is the most important factor. Someone who invests $300 a month for 30 years will usually end up with far more than someone who invests double that amount for only 15 years.

Tips

Automate your contributions, keep fees low, and avoid withdrawing early. Remember that stock market returns are not guaranteed and can be negative in some years. Savings accounts and CDs typically offer lower but more predictable rates.
