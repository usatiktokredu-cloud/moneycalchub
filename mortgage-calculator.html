How a mortgage payment is calculated

Your monthly mortgage payment has up to four parts, often called PITI: principal, interest, taxes and insurance. Principal and interest are fixed on a fixed-rate loan, while property tax and homeowners insurance can change from year to year.

Tips to lower your payment

A larger down payment reduces the amount you borrow. Shopping around with several lenders can lower your rate, and even a 0.25% difference adds up over 30 years. Choosing a 15-year term raises the monthly payment but saves a large amount of interest. If your down payment is under 20%, lenders usually require private mortgage insurance (PMI), which is not included in this estimate.

How much house can I afford?

A common guideline is to keep housing costs under about 28% of your gross monthly income and total debt payments under about 36%. Use the Debt-to-Income Calculator on this site to check your own ratio.
