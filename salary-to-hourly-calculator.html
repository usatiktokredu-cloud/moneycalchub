Salary to Hourly Calculator

Convert yearly salary to hourly, weekly and monthly pay.

Converting salary to an hourly wage

To find your hourly rate, divide your annual salary by the number of hours you work in a year. A full-time schedule of 40 hours for 52 weeks is 2,080 hours, so a $65,000 salary is about $31.25 per hour.

Why it matters

Comparing a salaried job with an hourly job, or evaluating a freelance rate, is easier when everything is in the same unit. Remember to count unpaid overtime if your salary role regularly requires more than 40 hours.

Gross vs. take-home

The numbers here are before federal, state and payroll taxes and benefits deductions, so your take-home pay will be lower.
