Debt-to-Income (DTI) Calculator

Calculate your DTI ratio, which lenders use to approve loans.

What is debt-to-income ratio?

Your DTI is your total monthly debt payments divided by your gross monthly income. Lenders use it to judge whether you can afford a new loan.

What is a good DTI?

Under 36% is generally considered good. Many mortgage programs allow up to about 43%, and some go higher with strong credit or savings. Lower ratios usually mean better rates.

How to improve your DTI

Pay down high-interest debt, avoid taking on new loans before applying for a mortgage, and look for ways to increase your income. Even small changes can move your ratio noticeably.
