Planning for retirement

Retirement planning depends on how much you save, how long your money grows and what return you earn. This calculator projects a future balance based on your inputs.

The 4% guideline

Many planners use the 4% rule as a rough starting point: withdrawing about 4% of your savings in the first year of retirement, then adjusting for inflation, has historically lasted around 30 years. It is a guideline, not a guarantee.

Ways to boost your savings

Contribute enough to get any employer 401(k) match, since it is effectively free money. Increase your contribution rate by 1% each year, and consider tax-advantaged accounts such as a 401(k) or IRA. Consult a licensed financial professional for personal advice.
