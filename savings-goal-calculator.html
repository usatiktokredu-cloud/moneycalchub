Turn a goal into a monthly number

Whether you are saving for a down payment, emergency fund, wedding or vacation, breaking the goal into a monthly amount makes it far easier to achieve.

Where to keep your savings

High-yield savings accounts and certificates of deposit (CDs) offer FDIC-insured safety, usually up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank. For goals more than five years away you might consider other options, but always understand the risks.

Staying on track

Set up automatic transfers on payday, name your savings accounts after the goal, and review your progress every quarter.
