See how long it takes to pay off your credit card debt.

Why credit card debt costs so much

Credit card interest compounds, and average APRs are far higher than most other loans. Paying only the minimum can keep you in debt for many years.

Two popular payoff strategies

The avalanche method targets the card with the highest APR first, which saves the most money. The snowball method pays off the smallest balance first, which builds motivation. Both work if you stay consistent.

Tips

Consider a 0% APR balance transfer offer (check the transfer fee), stop adding new charges while you pay down the balance, and put any extra income toward the card each month.
